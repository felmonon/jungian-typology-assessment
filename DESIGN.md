# TypeJung design system: The Reflection

This file is the working design system for typejung.com. It replaces the
"Pattern Studio" notes in `DESIGN-REFRESH.md` and `UI-UX-IMPROVEMENTS.md`.
Tokens live in `styles/globals.css`; components and page compositions live in
`styles/reflection.css`. Identity files are generated by
`brand/build-identity.py` into `public/brand/` (see Identity files below).

## Directions considered

Three genuinely different directions were explored before building. Each was
judged against what TypeJung actually is: a free 42-question map of all eight
cognitive functions, built around the dominant–inferior axis, with an
optional one-time report.

### 1. The Reflection (chosen)

- **Thesis.** Every function stack has a side you lead with and a side you
  are still growing toward. The site is organised around a horizon: what is
  conscious sits above the line, what is less developed hangs below it as an
  outline. Calm, exact, a little nocturnal.
- **Layout.** Swiss: one grid, left-aligned, large type, flat fields of
  colour. Pages move from a light field (the day side) into a night field
  (the side below the line) at the point where the content turns to stress,
  growth and depth.
- **Type and colour.** One family, Instrument Sans, semi-condensed for
  display. White ground, near-black ink, one signal red, and one night field.
  Red marks only the action and the dominant function.
- **Logo.** A solid red triangle standing on a horizon bar, with its outline
  reflected below: the dominant–inferior axis drawn as a mark.
- **Signature.** The horizon map: the person's real stack drawn with the
  dominant and auxiliary above the line and the tertiary and inferior below.
- **Fit and risks.** Fits the product's core idea and Jung's own image of
  consciousness as a lit surface over a deeper field. Risks: red can read as
  alarm, so it never carries warnings; the horizon must stay a solid shape,
  not a hairline.

### 2. Case notebook (rejected)

- **Thesis.** The result as a page from a reader's notebook on *Psychological
  Types* (1921): margin notes, index cards, a serif text face.
- **Logo.** A typed paper label with a "TJ" monogram.
- **Signature.** Hand-set marginalia beside each result section.
- **Why not.** It slides into the beige editorial template the brief warns
  against, it reads academic rather than personal, and margin notes collapse
  badly on phones, where most results are read.

### 3. Night instrument (rejected)

- **Thesis.** A dark interface with luminous dials; the eight functions on a
  radar plot that sweeps as answers come in.
- **Logo.** An eight-spoke dial.
- **Signature.** An animated dial on the result page.
- **Why not.** It implies measurement precision the assessment does not
  claim, a radar of eight values over-states eight derived numbers as
  independent measurements, and it lands close to the dark SaaS look.

**Why the Reflection wins.** It is the only direction whose central image is
the product's own idea (the dominant–inferior axis), so the logo, the result
visual, the buttons and the page rhythm all say the same thing. It also gives
the free map a complete, finished shape on its own, while the paid report can
be presented as "what lies below the line" without making the free result
look unfinished.

## Idea sentence

On this site, **the dominant–inferior axis** becomes **a horizon with a
reflection**: the logo, the bullet, the result map, the section band and the
home page's live example.

The live thing on the home page: pick the function you lean on most and see
which function sits below your line (its opposite). The pairs are standard
Jungian theory and the copy says it is a preview, not a result.

No monospace, no capitals with letter-spacing, no index numbering. Numbers
that align use `tabular-nums`.

Space: 4px base; page margins 16px (phone), 32px (tablet), 48px (desktop);
content max 1280px. Gaps inside a section stay below 48px; between sections
96–160px on desktop, 64–96px on phones.

Shape: square corners everywhere (`--radius-*` are 0). Circles are allowed
only for genuinely circular things (radio dots, avatars). No shadows at rest;
`--shadow-float` exists for menus and dialogs only. No gradients, no glass.

## The motif

- **Mark:** red triangle on a horizon bar, outline reflected below.
  `components/brand/TypeJungMark.tsx` renders it inline.
- **Horizon band:** a solid 6px ink bar across the content width with the
  mark's diamond centred on it. Used once per long page, where the content
  turns from the free map to depth (`.horizon-band`).
- **Bullet:** a small diamond, filled for things you get, outlined for
  optional things (`.list-diamond`).
- **Function glyph:** each of the eight functions is drawn on Jung's compass:
  thinking at the top, feeling at the bottom, sensation left, intuition
  right. Introverted functions fill their point inside the diamond;
  extraverted ones point outward from it. A dominant and its inferior are
  always mirror images (`components/brand/FunctionEmblem.tsx`).

## Components

- **Buttons** (`components/ui/Button.tsx`): square, 48px minimum (52px for
  `lg`), weight 600. `accent` is the signal red and appears once per screen.
  `primary` is ink. `outline` uses the control line. Hover darkens, press
  moves 1px down, focus shows a 3px ink outline offset by 3px. Loading
  replaces the right icon with a spinner and keeps the label ("Checking
  availability…").
- **Tiles** (`.tile`): a surface-colour block, no border, 24–32px padding.
  Never nested inside another tile.
- **Fields** (`.input-premium`): white fill, 1px control line, 48px tall,
  label above in Small. Focus: 2px ink border. Error: message below in error
  colour with an icon, `aria-describedby` linked.
- **Tabs** (result navigation, `.result-tabs`): text tabs on one line with
  a 4px ink bar under the active one; a four-column grid below 640px so no
  tab hides off-screen.
- **Disclosure** (`details`): filled tile, plus sign that turns to minus.

- **Page header** (`.page-head`, `.page-title`, `.page-lead`): left-aligned,
  for plain pages (About, Learn, Privacy, Terms). No icon above the title.

## Page patterns

- **Results** (`rs-*` in `styles/reflection.css`): the headline names the
  dominant function ("… leads your map."); the horizon map puts dominant over
  inferior and auxiliary over tertiary, bars growing from the line. The
  eight-function list scales bars to the highest score; only the dominant is
  red. The report offer is a night section (`.rs-offer`) with the price,
  one-time and refund lines beside the button and the "Not ready to decide?"
  email option under it. The share card (`HorizonCard`) is a static night
  tile.
- **Assessment** (`qz-*`): a segmented progress bar (one segment per
  question), the four chapters named under it, options as full-width faces
  with the number key shown on hover-capable screens, and a sticky action
  bar. Completion shows a night overlay ("Mapping your energy…").
- **Checkout review** (`co-*`): the saved map's dominant over inferior axis
  on the left, the payment card on the right (first on phones), every
  async state written out.
- **Reports** (`reader-*`): one column of parts ("Part 2 of 9"), a jump
  select on phones and a contents list on desktop.
- **Static SEO and blog pages**: share one stylesheet,
  `scripts/static-page-style.mjs`, injected by the generators. Change it
  there and regenerate (`npm run generate:static`, `npm run generate:blog`).

## Identity files

- `brand/build-identity.py` draws every SVG from one geometry: marks
  (colour, dark, mono ink, mono paper), wordmarks, lockups, favicon, app and
  maskable icons, and the 1200×630 social preview (`public/og-image.svg`).
  Text is outlined from Instrument Sans so the SVGs need no font.
- `node brand/render-rasters.mjs` renders the PNGs from those SVGs
  (favicons 16 and 32, apple-touch-icon 180, logo 512, og-image).
- Run both after any change to the mark, then bump the `?v=` on the icon
  links in `index.html`.

## Responsive rules

- Phones (375): one column, 16px margins, display type at about 11vw, the
  result map stacks with the horizon full width, primary actions full width
  and in thumb reach.
- Tablet (768): two columns only where both halves carry real content.
- Desktop (1440): 12-column grid, text measure 60–68 characters.
- No horizontal page scroll at any width; long words wrap
  (`overflow-wrap: anywhere` on user-generated strings).

## Interaction rules

- Motion uses transform and opacity only, 160–420ms, `cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.1,1)`.
  Section parts rise 16px and fade in once. Everything has a
  `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback that shows content immediately.
- The signature move: the horizon map's bars grow from the line when the
  map first appears.
- Every control has hover, active, focus-visible and disabled states.
- Async states: loading text ends with an ellipsis, errors say what to do
  next, success messages are calm and specific (no exclamation marks).

## Product guardrails

- The free map is complete on its own. The report is described as optional
  depth, with its price, one-time payment and refund policy next to the
  button, and the sample available before checkout.
- No invented testimonials, metrics or scientific claims. Diagrams draw only
  real result data or labelled illustrative examples.
- Analytics event names, checkout guard, report availability checks, URLs
  and SEO metadata are unchanged by design work.

## Quality gate (run before shipping a design change)

1. `npm test` and `npx tsc --noEmit` pass.
2. Screens at 375, 768 and 1440 show no horizontal overflow.
3. Keyboard: every action reachable, focus always visible. Tap targets are
   44px or larger outside running text.
4. Contrast: body text ≥ 4.5:1, large text and field edges ≥ 3:1. axe-core
   (WCAG 2.2 AA tags) reports no violations on the key routes.
5. Reduced motion: no content depends on an animation to appear.
6. No eyebrow labels, monospace, thin rules, shadows at rest or gradients on
   rebuilt pages.
