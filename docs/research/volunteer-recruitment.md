# TypeJung: five volunteer observation sessions

Status: recruitment prepared, not posted. The owner selected free public recruitment and will choose the community/account. No participants have been recruited or observed, and no payment or incentive has been promised.

## Public invitation

**Interested in personality tests? Five volunteers wanted for a website feedback session**

I’m working on TypeJung, a personality-assessment website, and I’m looking for five adults who are interested in personality tests to try it and share honest feedback.

This is an unpaid volunteer session lasting about 35–45 minutes. You’ll use the website on your own phone or computer, then talk about what felt clear, confusing or useful. No expertise is needed, and you won’t need to buy anything. I’m particularly interested in hearing from people who don’t know me personally.

Recording is optional and would only happen with your separate permission. You can stop at any time and don’t need to discuss your personal assessment answers.

If you’re interested, reply to this post. I’ll follow up with a few short screening questions and arrange a time privately. Please don’t post personal results or contact details publicly.

## Private screening after someone opts in

1. Are you 18 or older and comfortable using an English-language website?
2. What got you interested in personality tests recently? If you have used any, which ones? You do not need to share your results.
3. Have you used TypeJung before? If so, approximately when?
4. Would you normally take a test like this on a phone, tablet or computer?
5. Do you know the person building TypeJung personally, or have you worked on the product?
6. Are you comfortable with a 35–45 minute unpaid session in which someone observes your use of the site and asks a few questions afterward? Recording is a separate, optional choice.

Choose five people whose answers show a real reason for exploring personality tests. Prefer people who are new to TypeJung and are not friends or colleagues of its owner. Aim for three phone and two desktop sessions if those volunteers are available. Do not select based on willingness to buy, positive opinions of TypeJung, personality type, or agreeing to recording. Do not exclude someone solely for expressing skepticism.

Ask selected volunteers privately for their timezone and two or three available times. Confirm the meeting method and time before marking a session booked. Do not schedule more than five confirmed sessions or promise compensation.

## Session setup

- Use the current live offer and CA$7 price throughout the study. Do not coach navigation, offer an extra discount, or send the sample report beforehand.
- After consent, provide the tagged study entry link: `https://typejung.com/?utm_source=typejung_research&utm_campaign=offer_observation_2026_10`.
- Ask for a fresh private browser window for the session and have the participant close it afterward. Explain that this keeps research activity separate from their usual browsing. Do not ask them to disable privacy protections.
- The study link assigns a separate research browser identity for the tab. Customer conversion reports exclude marked study browsers; original stored records and actual revenue remain intact. Use the live link only after the research-exclusion release is verified.
- If browser restrictions prevent the research marker from surviving a full reload, pause before proceeding and record that the session needs manual exclusion. Do not treat an unverified session as ordinary customer traffic. No personal identifier belongs in the study URL.
- Use P1–P5 in observation notes. Keep opt-in contact details and scheduling separate from behavior notes.

Consent to observe: “May I observe you using the website and take notes about navigation and your feedback? You can stop or skip a question at any time. You do not need to share personal answers or payment details.”

Separate recording request, only if recording is actually needed: “Would you also be comfortable with a recording? Declining won’t affect your participation. Before we start, I’ll explain who can access it, where it will be kept, and when it will be deleted.”

The owner must specify access, storage and a deletion date before recording. If those are not settled, take notes only. Stop recording before any private assessment answers, account credentials, notifications or payment information become visible.

## Task and follow-up

Task: “Use TypeJung as you normally would. When you reach your result, explore it and decide what you would do next.”

Let them finish their natural exploration without pointing out the offer. Record what they notice and do before asking about buying. If they miss the offer, record that first; any later guided exposure is labeled prompted.

Then ask exactly:

- “What did you think the paid report would add?”
- “What made you consider buying or decide against it?”

If they never noticed the paid option, “I didn’t notice one” is a valid answer. Ask neutral follow-ups such as “What led you to that?” Do not defend the product or turn the session into a sales conversation. Do not require a purchase.
