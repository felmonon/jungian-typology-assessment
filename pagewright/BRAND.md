# Pagewright brand

**Pagewright** — *a maker of pages.* A local-first word processor for people who send documents
to other people.

**Promise:** *Write the page you'll send.* What you see is the page that prints, the file you
open is the file you keep, and nothing is lost without you being told first.

**Positioning:** for writers of letters, reports and proposals who exchange `.docx` files, Pagewright
is the desktop word processor that puts the page first and treats file fidelity as a feature you
can see, unlike suites that bury the page under toolbars and drop what they can't read without
saying so. *(Name is provisional; see "Name research". No trademark clearance has been done.)*

## Three directions considered

| | A. Pressroom — "Quoin" | B. Instrument — "Plumb" | **C. Page workshop — "Pagewright"** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Idea | Letterpress: the quoin is the wedge that locks set type so nothing shifts | Swiss precision: plumb means exactly true | A wright is a maker; the product makes real pages |
| Look | Newsprint, black ink, press red, slab serif, wedge-shaped Q | Cool greys, one signal orange, geometric grotesk, plumb-bob caret | Warm paper, ink, deep viridian, ochre fold, editorial serif with humanist UI |
| Clarity | Low: obscure word, unclear pronunciation ("koyn") | Medium: "true", but also plumbing | **High:** says *pages* and *craft* |
| Distinctiveness | High | Medium–high | **High in category** (no blue, no "Doc/Word/Writer" naming) |
| Credibility | Medium–high; "locked" suggests rigidity, wrong for drafting | High, but cold for writers | **High:** crafted, trustworthy, calm |
| Collisions found | Searches surface Quip (a word processor) for "Quoin"; a hobby "Quoin" document engine | No writing-app collision found | No product with this exact name found; "Penwright" (a Typst editor) shares the suffix |

**Selected: C, Pagewright.** It is the only direction whose name explains the product, its
visual language comes straight from the core promise (the page), and the warm viridian/ochre
palette is far from every incumbent's blue.

### Name research (2026-10-19, web searches; not legal advice)

Rejected for collisions: **Recto** (rectoapp.com, a document platform), **Stet** (a new open-source
local-first document editor; also historic editors), **Deckle** (a paid novel-writing app),
**Quire** (Getty's publishing framework; a task app), **Quarto** (Posit's publishing system),
**Vellum** (book-formatting app), **Platen** (a Flathub typesetting app). **Pagewright**: no
software product with this name found. Before any public release, run a proper trademark search
in the target markets.

## Logo

- **Mark — the Folded Leaf** (`brand/pagewright-mark.svg`, dark variant `-dark`): a page whose top
  right corner is folded down to show its ochre underside, with a text caret at the writing
  margin. 64-unit grid, 2.5-unit ink outline for use on UI surfaces.
- **Wordmark** (`brand/pagewright-wordmark.svg`): "Pagewright" in Source Serif 4 Semibold, −1.2 %
  tracking, outlined (no font needed). Lockups: `pagewright-lockup.svg`, `-lockup-dark.svg`.
- **App icon** (`brand/pagewright-app-icon.svg`, PNGs 16–1024 in `brand/png/`): viridian rounded
  square, paper page, ochre fold, tall caret: *a fresh page, ready to write.* A simplified
  `-small` drawing is used at 16–48 px.
- All of it is regenerated by `python3 brand/build_brand.py` from `brand/tokens.json`.
- Don't: put the mark on blue, add a letter to the page, recolour the fold, or stretch the page.

## Colour

Tokens live in [`brand/tokens.json`](brand/tokens.json); `crates/pagewright-ui/src/tokens.rs`
mirrors them and a unit test fails if they drift.

| Role | Light | Dark | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Desk | `#ECE8E0` | `#121413` | Canvas behind pages |
| Page | `#FFFFFF` | `#FFFFFF` | Pages stay paper in both themes: the screen matches print |
| Surface / raised | `#F7F5F0` / `#FFFFFF` | `#1B1E1D` / `#232726` | Bars, panels, menus |
| Text / muted | `#1D1B18` / `#68625A` | `#ECE8E1` / `#A8A299` | |
| **Viridian** (accent) | `#1E6A5A` | `#5DB49E` | Primary actions, focus, active state |
| **Ochre** (marker) | `#E8B931` | `#E8B931` | Find matches, the fold, "needs attention" |
| Warning / danger | `#946000` / `#B0402D` | `#E2B04A` / `#E58A77` | Fidelity warnings, destructive actions |

Text/background pairs used for text meet WCAG 2.2 AA (≥ 4.5:1), computed from the tokens and
checked by `tokens.rs` tests: ink on surface 15.8:1, muted on surface 5.5:1, white on viridian
6.4:1, viridian on surface 5.9:1, warning on its tint 4.7:1, danger on surface 5.3:1; dark theme:
text 13.8:1, muted 6.6:1, accent 6.8:1, text on accent 6.9:1. `textFaint` (3.0:1) is reserved for
disabled controls and decoration, which WCAG exempts; it never carries information.

## Space, shape, icons

- 4 px base: 2, 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32. Radii: 4 (chips), 6 (controls), 10 (panels), 14 (dialogs).
- Bars: title 44 px, format 40 px, status 26 px; outline sidebar 248 px; 24 px between pages.
- Icons: original, drawn in code (`crates/pagewright-ui/src/icons.rs`) on a 16 px grid, 1.5 px
  rounded strokes, no fills except state. No third-party or Microsoft icons.

## Voice

Plain, specific, calm. Say what happened and what the person can do: *"Saved to Documents ·
just now"*, *"1 item in this file can't be kept. Your original stays untouched unless you choose to
replace it."* No exclamation marks, no "Oops", no AI language, no claims of "replacing Word".
